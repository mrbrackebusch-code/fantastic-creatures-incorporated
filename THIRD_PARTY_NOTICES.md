# Third-party notices and source provenance

This tutorial's owner-authored game, explanation, and learner voice use programming-operation cadence from **Introduction to Computer Science with MakeCode Arcade / CS Intro 1 and CS Intro 2**, published by Microsoft MakeCode in the `microsoft/pxt-arcade` repository. The retained units include Button Speed (increment and timed events), Overlap Events, and the assignment/append and button-event examples in Arrays Intro.
